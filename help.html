使用幫助

ElementaWall 根據生辰八字，分析每個月的動態喜用和運勢變化，每月為你定制運勢能量手機桌布。以下是本站的主要操作說明。

1. 開始使用

在首頁點擊「點擊開始生成專屬手機桌布」，進入排盤頁。

未登入時會先顯示一份示例八字，標題旁帶有「（示例）」，方便你先瀏覽效果。右上角可切換繁體中文、简体中文與 English。

2. 登入與註冊

保存八字檔案、下載手機桌布、完成支付前，需要先登入。支援郵箱驗證碼登入／註冊、密碼登入／註冊，或使用 Google 登入。

3. 八字檔案

登入後點擊「+ 新增檔案」，填寫暱稱、性別、公曆出生日期與時間，並依序選擇出生國家／地區、省／州、城市。

建議開啟「真太陽時」，使時柱更準確；若出生時當地實行夏令時（時鐘撥快 1 小時），請同時開啟「夏令時」。

  • 保存後，四柱排盤、本月喜用與專屬手機桌布會依該檔案更新。
  • 用「切換檔案」可在多份檔案間切換，也可編輯或刪除。
  • 登入後若編輯並保存了示例檔案，它會成為你的真實檔案，「（示例）」標記會消失。
  • 已綁定包年的檔案不可修改或刪除。

4. 閱讀排盤

頁面中部為年柱、月柱、日柱、時柱。下方為大運、流年、流月。

若開啟真太陽時，會同時顯示校正後的時間與時鐘時間。

「本月最佳喜用」以原局四柱日主的旺衰格局為根基，綜合大運、流年的走勢與本月地支的刑沖合害，運用「扶抑、調候、通關、泄秀」等八字技法，選出最能疏通本月氣場、令命局動向最順遂的喜用五行。

「本月最佳補運」基於同一套月運推演，將喜用五行與忌神分別對應所主的生活領域，定位出本月氣場最宜補充、相對較弱的板塊，作為重點滋補與主動調和的方向。

5. 下載本月手機桌布

本月指的是流月（節氣月），非公曆月。「本月專屬手機桌布」會依喜用與補運給出對應圖。點擊「下載高清無水印原圖」後，以單張價格（US$5.99 / ¥39）直接支付；包年服務可直接下載指定八字檔案每月專屬手機桌布。

  • 支付成功後會自動開始下載；若失敗，可到右上角「用戶」→「下載記錄」重新下載。
  • 所有用戶均可使用 Stripe、PayPal；中國大陸境內用戶關閉 VPN 並重新整理頁面後，還可選擇支付寶支付。

6. 本月五行盲盒

本月指的是流月（節氣月），非公曆月。專屬手機桌布下方為「本月五行盲盒」。盲盒只看五行、不含補運，與上方 4 張專屬圖分屬兩套圖庫，不會抽到同一張。抽取必須先登入。每個帳號每流月最多開啟 3 次，同一帳號下多份八字檔案共用這 3 次。

  • 每次抽取同時開出 4 張；第 2、3 次會整組換成新的 4 張，頁面只展示當前這一組。
  • 五行從金、木、水、火、土完全隨機抽出。同一抽的 4 張圖不會重複；允許同一五行。
  • 非包年用戶：第 1 次免費開啟；第 2、3 次每次 US$0.99 / ¥6.5。開啟後下載原圖為 US$2.99 / ¥19。若該次為付費開啟，可用該筆開啟費抵扣一次，實付 US$2.00 / ¥12.5。第 3 次開啟後，若第 2 次尚未抵扣，可在同一張原圖上疊加兩筆開啟費，實付 US$1.01 / ¥6（即 US$2.99 − US$0.99 − US$0.99，¥19 − ¥6.5 − ¥6.5）。跨流月作廢。
  • 包年用戶：每個流月可免費開啟 3 次，每次開出的 4 張均可免費下載原圖。
  • 集齊金、木、水、火、土各至少 1 張付費下載的盲盒原圖，可兌換 1 次免費下載高清無水印原圖，可選當前盲盒 4 張或本月專屬 4 張之一。每套可領 1 次，可重複湊套；每張已購圖只計入一套。標題下方會顯示已集齊數量與已兌換張數。包年期間此兌換不適用；包年免費下載的盲盒原圖不計入集齊。
  • 下載記錄中，專屬桌布按流月顯示；盲盒記錄跨流月保留，可重新下載。

7. 包年服務

包年服務可指定兩個八字檔案全年免費使用本站所有付費服務。所有登入用戶均可在「用戶」→「包年服務」查看或購買。購買後可指定最多兩個八字檔案，也可稍後再選；綁定後這些檔案不可修改、不可刪除。已綁定的檔案可免費下載本月專屬手機桌布。五行盲盒按帳號生效：每個流月可免費開啟 3 次，每次開出的 4 張均可免費下載原圖，無需綁定檔案。包年期間不適用集齊五行兌換；未指定的檔案下載專屬原圖仍需付費。

8. 邀請碼獎勵（限時）

即日起至 2026 年 8 月 31 日，成功邀請 3 位新用戶完成註冊，可再免費下載一張帶水印的專屬手機桌布。邀請獎勵不能兌換無水印原圖。

登入後打開「用戶」→「邀請碼獎勵」，可填寫好友的邀請碼，或複製自己的邀請碼分享。活動截止後邀請機制取消，已獲得的帶水印免費下載仍然有效。

9. 帳號管理

在「用戶」→「帳號管理」可更改密碼或註銷帳號。註銷後帳號與雲端檔案無法恢復。其他問題可到「聯絡我們」寫信至 support@elementawall.com。

ElementaWall analyzes monthly shifting favorable elements and fortune changes from your BaZi chart, and customizes a fortune energy wallpaper for you each month. Here is how to use the site.

1. Getting started

On the home page, tap “Click to generate your exclusive wallpaper” to open the chart page.

When you are not signed in, an example chart is shown with “(Example)” next to the title so you can preview the experience. Use the top-right menu to switch among Traditional Chinese, Simplified Chinese, and English.

2. Sign in

You need to sign in before saving profiles, downloading wallpapers, or paying. Use an email verification code, a password, or Google Sign-In.

3. BaZi profiles

After signing in, tap “+ Add Profile” and enter a nickname, gender, Gregorian birth date and time, then choose country/region, province/state, and city.

We recommend turning on True Solar Time for a more accurate hour pillar. If daylight saving time was in effect at birth (clocks set 1 hour ahead), also turn on DST.

  • After saving, the four pillars, this month’s favorable elements, and wallpapers update for that profile.
  • Use “Switch Profile” to move between profiles, or to edit or delete one.
  • If you sign in and edit the example profile, it becomes your real profile and the “(Example)” label disappears.
  • Profiles bound to an annual plan cannot be edited or deleted.

4. Reading the chart

The middle of the page shows the Year, Month, Day, and Hour pillars, then Luck Cycle, Annual, and Monthly luck.

With True Solar Time on, you will see both the corrected time and clock time.

“Best Favorable Element” is rooted in the day-master’s strength and balance across the natal Four Pillars. It then weighs the current Luck Cycle and Annual luck against this month’s earthly-branch interactions—clashes, combinations, harms, and punishments—and applies classical BaZi techniques (supporting the weak, balancing climate, unblocking stagnation, and channeling excess) to identify the primary and secondary Five Elements that best clear this month’s energy and put the chart’s momentum in the most favorable direction.

“Best Supportive Boost” draws from the same monthly reading and maps each favorable and unfavorable element to its corresponding Ten God, then to the life affairs each Ten God governs. This pinpoints the domains where the chart’s energy is most depleted or under pressure from clashes and drains this month—the areas that benefit most from proactive reinforcement and intentional adjustment.

5. Download this month’s wallpapers

“This month” means the solar month (jieqi month), not the Gregorian calendar month. This Month’s Wallpapers are matched to your favorable elements and boosts. Tap Download HD Original (No Watermark) to pay for a single image (US$5.99 / ¥39). With an annual plan, you can download each month’s exclusive wallpapers for the bound BaZi profiles at no extra charge.

  • Downloads start automatically after payment. If one fails, go to User → Downloads to retry.
  • All users can pay with Stripe or PayPal. Users in mainland China can also choose Alipay after turning off VPN and refreshing the page.

“This month” means the solar month (jieqi month), not the Gregorian calendar month. Below the exclusive wallpapers is This Month’s Element Mystery Box. Boxes use Five Elements only (no boost tags) and come from a separate library, so they never overlap with the four exclusive images. You must sign in to draw. Each account may open the box up to 3 times per solar month; multiple BaZi profiles on one account share these 3 draws.

Credit expires at the end of the solar month.

  • Collect at least one paid mystery-box original of each element (Metal, Wood, Water, Fire, Earth). Annual free downloads do not count. Sets can be completed again; each purchased image counts toward only one set. Progress and redeemed counts appear under the section title. This redeem is unavailable while an annual plan is active.
  • In Downloads, exclusive wallpapers are listed by solar month; mystery-box records are kept across months and can be re-downloaded.
